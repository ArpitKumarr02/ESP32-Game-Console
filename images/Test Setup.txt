#Testing Setup

-ESP32 NODEMCU
-PASSIVE BUZZER (PIN 18)
-FEMALE RCA JACK (PIN 25) (ONLY COMPOSITE VIDEO data AND GND)
-BREADBOARD

Flash the sketch to the ESP32.
Connect GPIO25 to the composite video input of your CRT TV.
Connect a passive buzzer to GPIO18.
Power on the ESP32.

On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network:
SSID: ESP32-TV-OS
Password: CRTremote8
Open a browser and go to:
http://192.168.4.1
Use the on-screen D-pad + Select / Back buttons to navigate and play.
